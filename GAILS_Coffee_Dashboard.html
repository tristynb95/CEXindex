Coffee Experience Index v3 — Methodology

Overview

The CEI v3 is a 0–100 composite derived from standardised regression (R² = 0.576). Weights reflect standardised β coefficients against NPS. Percentile ranks are computed within each month’s cohort.

Components

Efficiency (35%) — Customer-rated perception of service efficiency (β = 0.344).

Drink Quality (35%) — Customer-rated drink quality (β = 0.342).

Friendliness (20%) — Customer-rated friendliness (β = 0.178). Significant at p = 0.013.

Timeliness (10%) — Inverted % of orders exceeding 5 mins. A high over-5-min rate signals staff struggling with pace, lack of urgency, or drinks not being ticked off once complete (β = −0.095).

CEI = (Efficiency pctile × 0.35) + (Drink pctile × 0.35) + (Friendliness pctile × 0.20) + (Timeliness pctile × 0.10)

🎯 Target Bakeries — Priority Support List

How to use this list

Bakeries are ranked from lowest CEI to highest within the Needs Attention (CEI < 25) and Developing (CEI 25–49) bands — the bakeries at the top need the most urgent support.

The Where to Focus column shows which single area is dragging a bakery’s score down the most compared to other bakeries. Start coaching conversations there first before tackling anything else.

Individual scores shown in red are in the bottom quarter of all bakeries.

Performance Trends — Target Bakeries Over Time

How to read this section

Each target bakery’s CEI and component scores are tracked across every available month. The Direction column compares the most recent month to the previous month. 3-Month Trend shows the overall trajectory over the last 3 months of data.

↑ Improving = score rose by 3+ points. ↓ Declining = score fell by 3+ points. ↔ Stable = within ±3 points.
