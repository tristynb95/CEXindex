Coffee Experience Index v4 — Methodology

Overview

The CEI v4.1 is a 0–100 composite that combines two complementary scores: a Relative CEI (percentile-ranked against peers within each month’s cohort) and an Absolute CEI (scored against company benchmark targets). The Relative CEI is the primary ranking tool; the Absolute CEI tracks progress against standards regardless of how other bakeries perform.

Relative CEI — Components & Weights

Overall Efficiency (35%) — Customer-rated perception of service speed.

Drink Quality (35%) — Customer-rated drink quality.

Friendliness (25%) — Customer-rated friendliness.

Barista Speed (5%) — Tiered speed score using both % within 3 min and % over 5 min. Heavily downweighted because completion times are self-reported via KVLink and routinely gamed (orders marked complete before collection). Retained at minimal weight as a directional signal rather than removed entirely.

Barista Speed = (% within 3 min) − (% between 3–5 min × 0.3) − (% over 5 min × 1.0)
Relative CEI = (Efficiency pctile × 0.35) + (Quality pctile × 0.35) + (Friendliness pctile × 0.25) + (Barista Speed pctile × 0.05)

Confidence Weighting

Low-volume bakeries produce noisier scores. CEI v4.1 applies regression to the mean based on response volume:

High (15+ responses): 100% own score. Medium (8–14): 85% own + 15% cohort average. Low (<8): 65% own + 35% cohort average.

This prevents bakeries with 3 responses from leaping to the top (or bottom) on thin data.

Absolute CEI — Benchmark Scoring

Each component is scored against the company target with escalating penalties for falling below the 90% benchmark. Meeting or exceeding 90% earns full marks. Dropping below triggers progressively harsher deductions:

90%+ → Score 100 (full marks)
85–89% → −4 pts per % below 90 (e.g. 85% → score 80)
80–84% → −6 pts per % below 85 (e.g. 80% → score 50)
75–79% → −6 pts per % below 80 (e.g. 75% → score 20)
<75% → −4 pts per % below 75 (e.g. 70% → score 0)

This means 90% is genuinely non-negotiable — a bakery at 80% on any metric will lose half its absolute score for that component, and one at 75% will be near zero. The penalty tiers ensure there’s no “close enough” to target.

Abs CEI = (Efficiency abs score × 0.35) + (Quality abs score × 0.35) + (Friendly abs score × 0.25) + (Coffee Efficiency abs score × 0.05)

What changed from v3?

1. Barista Speed downweighted (20% → 5%): KVLink completion times are self-reported and routinely gamed — baristas mark orders complete before collection to hit the KPI. With no way to regulate this, the data is unreliable. Barista Speed is retained at 5% as a directional signal but no longer drives the score. The freed weight is redistributed to customer-reported NPS survey metrics which cannot be gamed.

2. Confidence Weighting: Low-volume bakeries are now pulled toward the cohort average, preventing noisy data from distorting rankings.

3. Absolute CEI: A new parallel score benchmarked against company targets (90% across all components, including beverage delivery time which is already penalty-adjusted via the tiered speed score), so network-wide improvement is visible even when peer rankings stay flat.

4. Rebalanced Weights: Overall Efficiency and Drink Quality now carry 35% each, Friendliness 25% — the index is 95% driven by what customers actually report in NPS surveys.

🎯 Target Bakeries — Priority Support List

How to use this list

Bakeries are ranked from lowest CEI to highest within the Needs Attention (CEI < 25) and Developing (CEI 25–49) bands — the bakeries at the top need the most urgent support.

The Where to Focus column shows which single area is dragging a bakery’s score down the most compared to other bakeries. Start coaching conversations there first before tackling anything else.

Individual scores shown in red are in the bottom quarter of all bakeries.

Performance Trends — Target Bakeries Over Time

How to read this section

Each target bakery’s CEI and component scores are tracked across every available month. The Direction column compares the most recent month to the previous month. 3-Month Trend shows the overall trajectory over the last 3 months of data.

↑ Improving = score rose by 3+ points. ↓ Declining = score fell by 3+ points. ↔ Stable = within ±3 points.
