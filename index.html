Coffee Experience Index v4 — Methodology

The Coffee Experience Index uses a 0 to 100 score to tell us how well we're doing. We look at it in two ways.

Peer Score

Like a race. Every selected period, all bakeries line up and we rank them against each other. Finish 1st out of 20 and your score is near 100; finish last and it's near 0. It doesn't matter what the actual numbers are — only where you finished in the group.

Benchmark Score

Like a test with a pass mark of 90%. Whether every other bakery is above or below 90% doesn't change your result — you either hit the standard or you didn't.

Use Peer to see who's leading the pack. Use Benchmark to see if the whole network is genuinely hitting the standard.

The Recipe: What Makes Up the Score?

We believe what the customer tells us matters most. That's why 90% of your score comes directly from customer feedback:

  • Drink + Meal NPS (15%) — Would the customer recommend the experience?
  • Drink Quality (25%) — Did the customer love their coffee?
  • Friendliness (25%) — Did the customer feel welcome?
  • Efficiency (25%) — Did the customer feel they were served quickly?
  • Avg Wait Time (5%) — Average wait time for drinks. Full marks (100 points) for 1:55 (115s) or less, then a straight-line penalty down to 0 points at 2:00 (120s) or slower.
  • Coffee Efficiency (5%) — How fast the order was completed. We display the % under 2 mins (Target: 80% < 2 min) in the KPI cards and tables, but the index score uses a progressive 5-tier scale: 1.0x for under 2 mins, 0.5x for 2–3 mins, 0.25x for 3–4 mins, 0.1x for 4–5 mins, and a -5.0x penalty for orders over 5 mins. If orders over 5 mins reach 1.5%, the component score is halved; if they reach 2.5%, the component scores 0.

Keeping It Fair

If a bakery only gets 2 or 3 reviews in a selected period, one bad review could unfairly ruin their score, or one perfect review could push them to the very top. To be fair, if you don't have many reviews, we gently blend your score with the average to keep things balanced for everyone. For the current month, that blend only applies late in the month where a bakery's response count is far below the cohort.

Our Standards (Floor-Based Penalties)

Our standards are built on hitting specific benchmarks: 55 or higher for Drink + Meal NPS, 90% or higher for customer feedback (Drink Quality, Efficiency, Friendliness), 80% or higher for Coffee Efficiency, and a hard limit of 1:55 (115 seconds) or lower for Avg Wait Time. If a bakery meets or exceeds its benchmark, it receives full marks (100 points).

For the Benchmark Score, each scored KPI now has a floor. Scores between the benchmark and floor lose points in a straight line; scores at or below the floor receive 0 points for that component:

  • Drink + Meal NPS: 55 or higher = 100 points; 45 or lower = 0 points.
  • Drink Quality, Efficiency, Friendliness: 90% or higher = 100 points; 80% or lower = 0 points.
  • Avg Wait Time: 1:55 or faster = 100 points; 2:00 or slower = 0 points.

Example (90% Benchmark): Hitting 90% gives 100. A score of 85% gives 50. A score of 80% or lower gives 0.

Example (NPS Benchmark): Hitting 55 gives 100. An NPS of 50 gives 50. An NPS of 45 or lower gives 0.

KPI Card Status Thresholds

To help scan the dashboard quickly, each KPI card displays a color-coded status tag (Exceeding, On Target, Watch, or Below) based on specific performance bands for the selected period:

  • Net Promoter Score (NPS): Exceeding (>60), On Target (55–60), Watch (45–54), Below (<45).
  • SHINE Cards (Drink Quality, Efficiency, Friendliness): Exceeding (≥93%), On Target (90–92%), Watch (80–89%), Below (<80%).
  • Coffee Efficiency: Exceeding (>80%), On Target (70–80%), Watch (60–69%), Below (<60%).
  • Orders >5 Min: Exceeding (<0.5%), On Target (0.5–1.0%), Watch (>1.0–2.4%), Below (≥2.5%).

What's New in v4?

  1. Listening to the customer: We reduced the score for Coffee Efficiency from 20% down to 5%, and instead put that focus strictly on how the customer rated their speed and quality.
  2. Fairer rankings for everyone: We added the fairness adjustment for bakeries with very few reviews, so rankings aren't thrown off by just a few responses.
  3. A true standard to aim for: We added the Benchmark Score to make sure we are not just trying to beat each other week-over-week, but genuinely improving our service and meeting our company targets.
