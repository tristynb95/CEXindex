Coffee Experience Index v4 — Methodology

How Do We Measure Success?

The Coffee Experience Index (CEI) uses a 0 to 100 score to tell us how well we're doing. We look at this in two ways: Relative CEI (how you compare to other bakeries this month) and Absolute CEI (how you compare to GAIL's overall strict standards, regardless of everyone else).

The Recipe: What Makes Up the Score?

We believe what the customer tells us matters most. That's why 95% of your score comes directly from customer feedback:

  • Efficiency (35%) — Did the customer feel they were served quickly?
  • Drink Quality (35%) — Did the customer love their coffee?
  • Friendliness (25%) — Did the customer feel welcome?
  • Till Speed (5%) — How fast the order was completed on the till. We give this a small weight because it doesn't always reflect the true time it takes to prepare the order.

Keeping It Fair

If a bakery only gets 2 or 3 reviews in a month, one bad review could unfairly ruin their score, or one perfect review could push them to the very top. To be fair, if you don't have many reviews, we gently blend your score with the average to keep things balanced and fair for everyone.

Our Standards

Our standard is simple: we aim for 90% or higher on everything. If a bakery hits 90%, they get full marks. However, if a score drops below 90%, we apply strict point deductions. For example, an 80% score loses half its points, and anything under 75% gets almost zero.

This strict scoring means 'close enough' isn't good enough. 90% is our benchmark.

What's New in v4?

1. Listening to the customer: We reduced the score for till speed from 20% down to 5%, and instead put that focus strictly on how the customer rated their speed and quality.

2. Fairer rankings for everyone: We added the fairness adjustment for bakeries with very few reviews, so rankings aren't thrown off by just a few responses.

3. A true standard to aim for: We added the Absolute Score to make sure we are not just trying to beat each other week-over-week, but genuinely improving our service and meeting our company targets.
