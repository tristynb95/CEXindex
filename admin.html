Region Coffee Team

Regions are detected automatically from the site directory. Start typing a colleague’s name, then choose their account to assign every bakery in that region. If a Coffee Partner or Coffee Trainer leaves and their patch is split, switch Cover on for the region and name whoever is covering each ops area — anywhere you leave blank stays with the region’s own pair above. Switch Cover off once a permanent replacement is in post and the whole region goes back to them.

Area Head Baristas

Every ops area detected in the site directory, grouped under its region. Name the Area Head Barista who looks after each one and the bakery they normally work at — an area can have more than one, so use + and − to add and remove rows. Each person stays with their home bakery when a new site directory is uploaded, so areas can be renamed, split or merged without losing anyone.
